I'm a sophomore at Rutgers University studying Computer Science. I ship AI-integrated SaaS — full-stack, opinionated, end-to-end. Architecture decisions, code, deploy. All mine.

My current focus is Quarry, a multi-model research engine for journalists with contradiction detection, source provenance, and a custom finance query language (QFL). Before that, I built PDFGeek during my software engineering internship at TrialX, and FileGeek, the document intelligence platform Quarry evolved out of.

I'm also building Engel, a SwiftUI iOS journaling app structured as a digital examen rather than a mood tracker.

I write a recurring column on technology and AI policy for Rising Kashmir and have published on News18, mostly on unintended consequences in tech and national security.

My workflow is Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, and Warp — spec-first, one feature at a time, plan mode before implementation.
